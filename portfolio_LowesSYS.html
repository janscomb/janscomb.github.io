Objective

In Q1 2025, my team and I were tasked with leveraging Milo AI to help both DIY and Pro customers visualize landscaping, patio, cabana, and pergola designs using real Lowe’s products. Every output from the experience featured shoppable items available at Lowe’s, creating a seamless path from inspiration to purchase. To bring this innovation to life, our product and design leadership team traveled to SXSW in Austin, TX, where we transformed a rented home into an immersive demo space. Over the course of the event, we guided 500+ attendees through a working prototype—an overwhelmingly positive response that validated customer interest and the tool’s potential.

Goals:

  • Drive Customer Engagement Through Immersive Visualization
    Enable DIY and Pro customers to easily visualize outdoor projects using Milo AI, increasing inspiration, confidence, and time spent interacting with Lowe’s products.

  • Increase Product Discovery and Conversion
    Seamlessly connect design outputs to shoppable Lowe’s products, turning design exploration into a direct path to purchase and boosting sales in outdoor categories.

  • Validate Market Fit Through Real-World Testing
    Use live customer feedback from SXSW and prototype engagement to assess product-market fit, guide future iterations, and inform broader rollout strategies.
