Objective

In Q3 2024, Lowe's formed a strategic partnership with Planta, a leading native app based in Sweden known for its AI-powered plant care and diagnostic technology. Through this collaboration, Lowe's integrates Planta’s care data and AI analysis into its digital experiences and promotes the Planta app to U.S. customers. In return, Planta directs its U.S. users to Lowe’s for recommended products related to plant care and maintenance—unlocking a potential $125 million market opportunity.

Goals:

  • Educate customers on diagnosing and caring for live goods using trusted plant science.
  • Connect plant care and maintenance needs directly to relevant Lowe's products.
  • Leverage Planta’s U.S. user base to drive traffic and conversion to Lowe’s digital channels.
