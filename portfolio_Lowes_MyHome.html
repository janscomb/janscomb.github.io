Objective

In Q1 2025 my team and I were challenged with utilizing our Milo AI to help our DIY and Pro customers envision landscaping, patio, and cabana / pergula designs. All the outputs provide products sold in Lowe's stores. Myself and our partnering Product Manager leaders went to Austin, TX for SXSW where we rented a home to give attendees an opportunity to try this tool on the backyard, it was a great success; we walked around 500 attendees through a working prototype.

Goals:

  • Teach 400,000+ employees about their benefits
  • Help them all make good decisions for themeselves and family
  • Cut down on HR call center calls
